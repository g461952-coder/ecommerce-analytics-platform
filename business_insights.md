1. Revenue and order value
R$13.59M
The dataset generated approximately R$13.59 million in product revenue across 99,441 orders, with an average order value of R$136.68.
Business implication: compare category performance and order value to identify opportunities for increasing sales.


2. Delivery performance
6.8% late deliveries
Around 6.8% of delivered orders were classified as late, and average delivery time was about 12.6 days.
Business implication: investigate late-delivery patterns by seller, destination, and product category before deciding where to improve logistics.


3. Customer satisfaction
4.09 / 5 average review
The overall average review score was approximately 4.09. Late deliveries had a substantially lower average review score than orders delivered on time or early.
Business implication: investigate delivery delays as a possible contributor to customer dissatisfaction. This is an association, not proof of causation.


4. Customer retention
About 3.1% repeat customers
The repeat customer rate is low when customers are identified using customer_unique_id and repeat purchasing is defined as placing more than one order.
Business implication: investigate customer retention and repeat-purchase opportunities. Confirm the calculation's customer and date definitions before presenting it.


5. Payments
Payment transaction value totals approximately R$16.01 million, compared with R$13.59 million in product revenue. These totals measure different things, so they should not be presented as contradictory results.
Business implication: compare payment methods and installment behavior to understand how customers pay.
